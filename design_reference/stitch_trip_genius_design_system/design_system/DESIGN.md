## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an editorial, high-end travel journalism aesthetic fused with modern, utilitarian artificial intelligence. The design narrative rejects hyper-digital tropes—such as neon gradients, heavy dark modes, or synthetic glassmorphism—in favor of organic restraint, physical print materiality, and natural warmth.

### Core Philosophy
- **Sophisticated & Literary**: Typography and pacing evoke independent travel volumes (like *Cereal* or *Monocle*), balancing generous negative space with authoritative typographic scale.
- **Trustworthy & Pragmatic**: Data-dense itinerary segments, logistical schedules, and travel confirmations are articulated with structured clarity, high-contrast legibility, and crisp structural borders.
- **Curious & Adventurous**: Editorial imagery, warm natural ivory paper-like surfaces, and earth-driven terracotta accents celebrate wanderlust without feeling gimmicky or transactional.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by forest foliage, raw clay, warm paper fibers, and weathered stone.

### Color Tokens & Assignments
- **Primary (`#244A3A`)**: Used for authoritative actions, key visual anchors, primary buttons, active state indicators, and prominent section banners.
- **Deep Forest (`#18372C`)**: Reserved for dark mode inversions, deep footer blocks, header navigation bars on immersive screens, and pressed states of primary elements.
- **Secondary / Accent (`#C87555`)**: Terracotta highlights for time-sensitive highlights, alert chips, itinerary pin markers, active booking nudges, and focal callout badges.
- **Tertiary / Soft Sage (`#DCE5DA`)**: Tonal surface fills, selected card backdrops, subtle tags, and muted status indicators.
- **Muted Sand (`#EDE6D9`)**: Neutral fill for secondary containers, table headers, hover states, and structural dividers.
- **Warm Ivory Base (`#F7F5EF`)**: The global page canvas background, lending a bespoke paper-like tactility that reduces digital eye fatigue.
- **Surface White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure elevated surface for interactive cards, sheets, dialogs, and form input backgrounds.
- **Charcoal Text (`#252923`)**: Primary text token providing high-contrast readability against both ivory and white surfaces.
- **Secondary Text (`#777A70`)**: Subtext, metadata timestamps, flight leg markers, and micro-labels.
- **Subtle Border (`#E4E2D9`)**: Structural 1px boundary lines establishing structural separation without visual weight.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies on a dynamic tension between the literary elegance of **DM Serif Display** and the crisp, geometric ergonomics of **DM Sans**.

### Usage Guidelines
- **DM Serif Display**: Reserved strictly for high-impact editorial headings, city titles, trip theme titles, day-by-day milestone breaks, and narrative modal headers. It must never be used below 22px to avoid legibility degradation.
- **DM Sans**: Handles all interface chrome, interactive inputs, itinerary step details, flight matrices, metadata tags, and body prose.
- **Uppercase Metadata**: Small labels and status tags use `label-md` or `label-sm` with slight positive tracking (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) and sentence/uppercase casing for clear scan-ability on schedules.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a balanced fluid-grid structure that respects classical editorial margins while accommodating multi-column logistics.

### Breakpoints & Grid Model
- **Desktop (1440px max-width)**: 12-column grid, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 48px (`3rem`) page margins. Optimized for side-by-side day planners, map splits, and drag-and-drop itinerary rails.
- **Laptop (1280px)**: 12-column grid, 20px gutters, 32px margins.
- **Tablet (768px)**: 8-column grid, 16px gutters, 24px margins. Navigation collapses to a top bar with drawer; itinerary days switch to stacked collapsible accordions.
- **Mobile (390px)**: 4-column grid, 12px (`0.75rem`) gutters, 16px (`1rem`) margins. Timeline steps stack into a single vertical stream with sticky bottom navigation.
- **Small Mobile (320px)**: 4-column grid, 8px gutters, 12px margins. Secondary labels and chip groups wrap aggressively.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system is tactile, flat-to-warm, and architectural. Rather than artificial glowing blurs or dramatic multi-layer drop shadows, spatial hierarchy is articulated through tonal stratification and delicate, warm ambient shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas)**: Warm Ivory (`#F7F5EF`). Never casts a shadow.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Containers / Sections)**: Muted Sand (`#EDE6D9`) or Soft Sage (`#DCE5DA`) resting directly on ivory with no shadow, bounded by a 1px Subtle Border (`#E4E2D9`).
- **Level 2 (Elevated Interactive Surfaces)**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) cards and floating itinerary blocks. Uses a warm ambient shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(37, 41, 35, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(37, 41, 35, 0.02);` bordered with 1px `#E4E2D9`.
- **Level 3 (Dropdowns & Popovers)**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a deeper architectural shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 10px 24px -4px rgba(37, 41, 35, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(37, 41, 35, 0.04);`
- **Level 4 (Modals & Sheets)**: Centered White card layered over a Charcoal backdrop with 40% opacity (`rgba(37, 41, 35, 0.4)`), applying:
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -8px rgba(24, 55, 44, 0.14);`

## Shapes

The design uses a dual-tier radius scale to reflect both technical precision and architectural calm.

### Radius Specifications
- **Controls & Small Components (Inputs, Buttons, Chips, Badges)**: 6px to 8px (`rounded-md`). Gives interactive controls a defined, grounded shape without appearing juvenile or overly pill-shaped.
- **Containers, Cards, & Panels**: 12px to 16px (`rounded-lg` / `rounded-xl`). Applied to trip itinerary cards, day itinerary columns, route summaries, and flight cards.
- **Pills**: Reserved solely for status badges (e.g., "Confirmed", "AI Suggested", "Fastest Route") using full circular radii (`rounded-full`).

## Components

All components must incorporate Lucide line icons (stroke width: 1.75px) in either Charcoal (`#252923`) or Primary Forest Green (`#244A3A`).

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#244A3A`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `8px`, height `44px` (desktop) / `48px` (mobile). Hover: `#18372C`. Active: scales down to `0.98`.
- **Secondary**: Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#244A3A`, 1px border `#E4E2D9`, border-radius `8px`. Hover: background `#EDE6D9`.
- **Terracotta Accent (Call-to-Action)**: Background `#C87555`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `8px`. Hover: `#b86749`.
- **Ghost**: Background transparent, text `#244A3A`, hover background `#DCE5DA` with 50% opacity.

### Chips & Filters
- **Default Chip**: Height `32px`, border-radius `8px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#E4E2D9`, text `#252923`.
- **Selected Chip**: Background `#244A3A`, text `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#244A3A`.
- **AI Recommendation Tag**: Background `#DCE5DA`, text `#18372C`, border 1px transparent, paired with a small `Sparkles` Lucide icon.

### Cards & Itinerary Items
- **Itinerary Activity Card**: Surface `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#E4E2D9`, corner radius `14px`, padding `16px`. Left-hand accent indicator: 3px solid bar colored either in Primary Forest Green (transportation/culture) or Terracotta (culinary/dining).
- **Day Bucket Container**: Background `#F7F5EF` with 1px border `#E4E2D9`, radius `16px`, padding `20px`. Header features `DM Serif Display` day title.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Height `44px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#E4E2D9`, corner radius `8px`, padding `0 14px`, text `#252923`, placeholder `#777A70`. Focus state: 1.5px border `#244A3A`, outline none, zero fuzzy focus rings.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size `18px`, radius `4px` (checkbox) / `50%` (radio). Unchecked: border 1.5px `#777A70`, background `#FFFFFF`. Checked: background `#244A3A`, border-color `#244A3A`, check icon in `#FFFFFF`.

### Timeline & Transit Path
- Vertical connecting line: 2px dashed `#E4E2D9`. Nodes: 10px circular dots filled with `#FFFFFF` and a 2px `#244A3A` rim.
