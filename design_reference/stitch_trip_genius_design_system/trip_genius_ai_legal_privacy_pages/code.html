Account Confidentiality

Trip Genius AI grants you a revocable, non-exclusive, non-transferable, and limited license to access our platform solely for personal, non-commercial travel planning and logistical exploration.

Section 04

4. User-Provided Information

The precision of generated itineraries directly reflects the integrity of the data you provide. You agree to submit accurate, up-to-date constraints regarding travel dates, party headcount, dietary restrictions, mobility requirements, and target expenditure levels.

We accept no liability for missed connections, unsuitable venue suggestions, or itinerary dysfunctions stemming from inaccurate or outdated prompt criteria provided by users.

Section 05

Trip Genius AI provides algorithmic assistance and editorial synthesis. All generated daily agendas, route segments, walking durations, and suggested points of interest constitute advisory guidance rather than guaranteed itineraries.

Real-world factors—including unexpected transit strikes, adverse weather, sudden municipal closures, or ticketing capacity—remain beyond the purview of predictive modeling. You acknowledge that independent verification of critical milestones is an essential obligation of individual travel.

Privacy Policy

How Trip Genius AI collects, manages, and shields your personal information across our intelligent travel planning architecture.

Section 01

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information directly when you register an account, configure vacation itineraries, or interact with our conversational itinerary architect. This comprises identification data, travel preference indicators, and device telemetry.

Section 02

2. Account and Authentication Information

Authentication credentials, including email addresses, encrypted password hashes, and federated login tokens (such as Google Identity), are managed securely via Supabase. We do not inspect or store unencrypted passwords.

Section 03

3. Travel Preferences and Trip Details

To formulate authentic expeditions, we process destination preferences, party composition, flight arrival timelines, dietary preferences, mobility requirements, and target budgets. This information is tied to your account to enable multi-device sync and retrospective travel journaling.

Section 04

4. How We Use Information

We process your data exclusively to:

  • Generate tailored, paced, and geographically sensible day-by-day itineraries.
  • Calculate realistic walking corridors and transit transitions via mapping APIs.
  • Notify you of critical disruptions or itinerary modifications you have requested.
  • Audit system performance and monitor model hallucination rates.
Section 05

5. AI Processing and Third-Party Providers

Zero Training on Personal Identifiers

When you submit prompts, your trip parameters (e.g., "4 days in Kyoto with high food focus") are sanitized to strip direct personal identifiers before transmission to underlying LLM APIs (such as Anthropic Claude or Google Gemini). Enterprise agreements prohibit third-party model providers from using your private travel queries to train public foundation models.

Section 06

6. Data Storage and Security

All structured records are preserved in enterprise-grade PostgreSQL databases hosted in secure cloud availability regions. Data is protected using TLS 1.3 encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest. Regular cryptographic audits ensure resilience against unauthorized intrusions.

Section 07

7. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your travel diaries or contact details to third-party data brokers or marketing conglomerates.

Disclosures are restricted to operational cloud infrastructure providers (hosting, transactional emails) who are contractually bound to process data strictly under our directions.

Section 08

8. Cookies and Local Storage

We employ necessary session cookies and browser LocalStorage to preserve your logged-in state, remember itinerary draft revisions during network drops, and retain user display preferences. We do not employ third-party cross-site advertising cookies.

Section 09

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Your travel histories remain accessible as long as your account is active. When you trigger an account deletion from your profile dashboard, your personally identifiable information is purged completely from active databases within thirty (30) days.

Section 10

10. User Choices and Privacy Rights

Regardless of your residency, Trip Genius AI adheres to rigorous standards inspired by GDPR and CCPA:

  • Right to Access: Export a JSON archive of all saved itineraries and preferences.
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate or outdated trip records instantly.
  • Right to Erasure: Permanent removal of your profile and history upon request.
Section 11

11. Children's Privacy

Our services are strictly directed to travelers aged 18 and older. We do not intentionally harvest data from children under the age of 16. If we discover inadvertent collection of such data, we take immediate steps to delete the records.

Section 12

12. International Data Transfers

To provide seamless global planning, information may be transferred to and maintained on cloud nodes located outside your home jurisdiction, including within the United States. All transfers are governed by standard contractual clauses ensuring equivalent safeguards.

Section 13

13. Changes to This Policy

Any updates to privacy procedures will be logged in this document with a modernized revision stamp. Significant shifts in processing philosophy will prompt a confirmation modal when you next log in.

Section 14

14. Contact and Privacy Requests

To exercise any privacy rights, request an archive export, or contact our Data Protection Officer:

Travel Planning Disclaimer

Critical disclosures regarding algorithmic itineraries, estimated budgets, local conditions, and traveler responsibilities.

Section 01

1. AI-Generated Recommendations

All itineraries, day-by-day narratives, restaurant selections, and cultural walk pairings provided by Trip Genius AI are synthesized by automated algorithmic models. While optimized for authenticity and logistical efficiency, they are intended exclusively as planning aids and brainstorming tools.

Local Realities Can Shift

Restaurants alter reservation policies, neighborhoods experience unexpected construction, and seasonal schedules change without immediate notice in digital databases. Travelers should verify vital appointments with venues directly.

Section 02

2. Estimated Costs and Budgets

Budget figures, average meal costs, transit fares, and museum admission prices displayed in Trip Genius AI are approximations based on historical medians and seasonal benchmarks.

They do not include unexpected currency exchange fluctuations, local city tourist occupancy taxes, surge rideshare pricing, holiday surcharges, or gratuities. Trip Genius AI does not guarantee that your actual expenditure will match calculated estimates.

Section 03

3. Routes and Travel Times

Walking times and transit intervals represent calculated estimates under ideal conditions. Real-world travel durations can be significantly lengthened by crowd volume, subway service suspensions, weather incidents, steep topography, or personal traveling speed.

Always allocate conservative transit buffers when connecting to high-consequence departures (such as international flights or high-speed rail lines).

Section 04

4. Opening Hours and Availability

Historical landmarks, imperial gardens, museums, and temples frequently enforce seasonal closures, private ceremonies, religious observances, or mandatory pre-booked entry tickets that cannot be monitored instantaneously by external AI models.

Travelers are urged to check official government or venue portals to secure timed tickets in advance.

Section 05

5. Safety and Accessibility

Trip Genius AI cannot evaluate real-time street-level safety, night lighting conditions, or granular wheelchair accessibility of uneven historical cobblestones.

You are solely responsible for reviewing official foreign ministry or embassy travel advisories, understanding regional health warnings, acquiring required immunizations, and validating step-free routes if traveling with limited mobility.

Section 06

6. Third-Party Bookings

Trip Genius AI is a planning engine, not an airline, lodging proprietor, or tour operator. When you purchase accommodations, flights, or tickets through external links, your financial contract is held directly with that respective merchant.

We accept no liability for third-party cancellations, overbookings, strike cancellations, or refund disputes.

Section 07

7. Final Travel Decisions

The Traveler's Sovereignty

Ultimate responsibility for personal health, visas, passport validity (ensuring 6 months remaining before expiration), travel insurance, physical readiness, and on-the-ground itinerary adaptations rests entirely with the individual traveler.
